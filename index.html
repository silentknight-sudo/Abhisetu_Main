How we work

From site visit to handover

01

Site visit

We inspect the property, take measurements and check wall condition and dampness.

02

3D design & quote

You see the room in 3D and get an itemised quote for material and labour.

03

Material selection

We help you choose paints, laminates and fittings to suit budget and surface.

04

Preparation

Furniture and floors are covered. Surfaces are repaired, filled and primed.

05

Execution

Trained teams do the work while a supervisor checks quality at every stage.

06

Clean-up & handover

We clean the site, walk through it with you and finish any touch-ups.
